# Folio privacy policy

_Last updated: 14 November 2026_

Folio is a PDF editor that works entirely on your device. This policy covers Folio for Windows, macOS, iPhone,
iPad and Android.

## What Folio collects

**Nothing.** Folio has no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting. It does not
send your documents, your signatures, your digital IDs or any information about you or your device anywhere.

## How your information is handled on your device

- **Documents** you open are read from where you keep them and are only written when you save or export, to the
  place you choose (on mobile, through the system share sheet). Folio does not keep its own copies, apart from
  temporary files the system creates while you work, which are deleted.
- **Signatures and initials** you draw or type are kept in Folio's local storage on that device so you can reuse
  them. You can delete them from the signature dialog at any time.
- **Digital IDs** (certificates for signing) are stored protected by the password you choose. On iPhone and iPad
  they are kept in the iOS Keychain, and on Android in storage encrypted with a key held by the Android Keystore;
  they are not synced or backed up off the device. On Windows and macOS they are kept in Folio's application data
  folder.
- **Text recognition (OCR)** runs on your device.

## Network access

Folio's desktop app blocks all network requests, and the mobile apps load nothing from the internet. The only way
information leaves your device is when you choose to share or save a file somewhere, for example to a cloud
folder or an email, using your device's own sharing features. That service's own privacy policy then applies.

## Children

Folio does not collect information from anyone, including children.
